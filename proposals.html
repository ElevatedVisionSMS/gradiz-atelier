Questions

Proposal questions

How much does a proposal setup cost in Miami?

Gradiz Atelier’s Marry Me and Be My Girlfriend proposal packages start at $449 for Intimate Yes, $649 for Signature Yes and $849 for The Gradiz Moment. Your location and add-ons can change the total, which is confirmed in a written quote before you book.

Where can you set up the proposal?

At home, on the beach, in a park or anywhere else you choose in Miami-Dade. Setups outside our local delivery area are quoted with the added travel. Some beaches and parks restrict balloons and other decorations, so we confirm what’s allowed at your spot and adjust the setup before you book.

What about permits and weather for a beach or park proposal?

We can secure the beach or park permit for you; the permit fee is added to your invoice with a $25 processing fee, or you can secure it yourself and skip that fee. If weather makes the setup unsafe or unworkable, you can rebook on another available date at no additional charge.

Are the flowers and candles real?

The red rose petals are artificial and the candles are LED. The Gradiz Moment includes a small fresh bouquet, and fresh flowers can be added to any package.
